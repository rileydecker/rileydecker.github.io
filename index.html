About me

I am a PhD candidate at SUNY Albany working in applied topology, and my advisor is Mike Lesnick. I'm interested in problems involving (but not limited to) multiparameter persistence, combinatorial and representation-theoretic aspects of TDA, sheaf theory, and network science.
